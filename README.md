## Story

The night before the NBA Finals, your basketball career hangs in the balance. The opposing team's manager, desperate to win, casts a dark spell that traps you in a mystical realm. Time is running out, and the game is about to start.

**Your mission:** Escape through five increasingly challenging levels by dunking basketballs through magical hoops. Master gravity-defying abilities, defeat cursed opponents, and make it back in time for tip-off.

---

## ✨ Features

### Core Gameplay
- **Precision Platforming** - Navigate challenging 2D puzzle rooms with tight controls
- **Basketball Physics** - Realistic ball throwing mechanics with arc trajectory
- **Dunk to Progress** - Score through hoops to unlock the next level

### Unique Abilities
- **Gravity Flip** - Reverse gravity (player only) to reach impossible platforms
- **Ball Teleport** - Throw the basketball and instantly teleport to its location
- **Progressive Unlocks** - Gain new abilities as you advance through your career

### Career Progression
Progress through your basketball journey across 5 unique locations:

| Level | Location | Challenge | Difficulty |
|-------|----------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | Moycullen Court | Spike Traps | ⭐ |
| 2 | Endas hall | Rising Water | ⭐⭐ |
| 3 | NUIG Kingfisher arena | Enemy mobs | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| 4 | Duke Stadium | Time Pressure | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 5 | NBA Finals | Ultimate Test | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

### 🎯 Difficulty Scaling
- Dynamic difficulty increases by **33% per level**
- Faster enemies, tighter time limits, and complex puzzles
- Designed for both casual players and platformer veterans

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Unity 6000.2.6f2 or newer
- Git
- 2GB free disk space

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository**
```bash
git clone https://github.com/yourusername/the-finals-curse.git
cd the-finals-curse
```

2. **Open in Unity**
- Launch Unity Hub
- Click "Add" → Select the project folder
- Open the project

3. **Open the first scene**
- Navigate to `Assets/_Project/Scenes/`
- Double-click `Level_01.unity`

4. **Play!**
- Press the Play button in Unity Editor
- Or build the project for your target platform
